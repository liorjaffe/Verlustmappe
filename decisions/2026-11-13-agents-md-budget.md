# 2026-11-13 - AGENTS.md size guard

The owner approved replacing the 80-line limit for `AGENTS.md` with a limit of 8,200
Unicode characters including newlines and 100 characters per line. The separate
60-line limit for `START.md` remains unchanged.

The line-only limit encouraged unreadably long lines. The new budget was calibrated once
from the reviewed, reformatted draft of 7,431 characters plus approximately 10% reserve.
It is durable; future increases require an owner decision and rationale.
