# 2026-11-12 – Report perspectives

The owner wants the case report to distinguish the complete PV-start energy path, the
EL-start portion of that path, and the auxiliary energy associated with the whole chain.
These perspectives support different evaluations without treating overlapping paths as
additive or counting embedded consumption twice.

The applicable boundaries are in `CONTRACT.md` §4. This decision does not define headline
formulas, change the A/B allocation or decide the proposed C formula.
